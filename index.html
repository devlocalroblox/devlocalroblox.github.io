What I Build

Every system is designed to hold up under real pressure: high CCU, exploiters, low-end devices, and months of heavy updates you didn't plan for.

01

Core Architecture

Modular, OOP-based frameworks written to be extended, not rewritten. Structure that makes sense six months after you last touched it.

02

Data Handling

ProfileStore, DataStore2, and custom-made wrappers with server-side authority and proper session locking to prevent data loss.

03

Security & Anti-Exploit

Every critical action validated server-side. RemoteEvent input sanitized, rate-limited, and rejected when it doesn't make sense.

04

Performance

Profiler-guided optimization. High frame rates under load, controlled NetworkPing, and no unnecessary replication.

05

UI/UX Systems

Clean, responsive GUIs with polished tween behavior. Dynamic menus that feel like they belong in the game, not bolted on after.

06

Networking

Clear client/server boundaries. Efficient RemoteEvent and RemoteFunction pipelines where each side owns exactly what it should.

07

Monetization

DevProducts, Gamepasses, and receipt handlers wired correctly; purchase flows tied to actual game economy logic.

08

Systems Design

Gameplay loops and progression systems built around long-term retention, not just the initial launch rush.

09

Debugging & Reliability

Edge cases caught before they ship. Complex bugs traced to their root cause, not papered over with workarounds.

Payment Terms

No upfront payments. Payment is handled one of three ways:

  1. Milestones - pay after each core deliverable is approved
  2. Time-based - daily, weekly, or monthly billing
  3. Upon completion - full payment after everything is done; requires the most background verification before starting

Background check and proof of payment is required before development begins. Message me your preference and I'll confirm from there. Background check is done by me and doesn't take long. The process is designed to be safe and smooth.

Percentage Deals

Revenue share is accepted under specific conditions:

  • A) Your game is already generating revenue; requires proof of analytics
  • B) Your game has legitimate revenue expectations; requires proof of ad spend or an existing fanbase, plus a short interview. Pure rev-share on new games with serious potential is open for discussion.
  • C) Percentage paired with a fixed payment, to reduce your upfront cost; exact split depends on scope and proof of revenue potential

Percentages range from 5% to 25%+ depending on the deal.
